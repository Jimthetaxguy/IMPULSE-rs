## 4. AutoPILOT (2611.28202), cost half only: no fit for the picker; this is Ion and tool-loop territory

The cost idea is to let the model write a small closed loop and call the model again only when that loop hands control back. That does not change `decide_step_model`. The Ion tool loop calls the model on every round: each round builds a `ChatRequest` whose `model` comes from `resolve_step_model` (confirmed on main 921f5c9; at 2ba676d this is `impulse-rs/src/llm_backends/mod.rs:837-844`). `LoopBudget` caps those model round trips by count and wall-clock (`max_rounds` and `wall_clock` at `impulse-rs/src/loop_contract.rs:94` and `:96` on main 921f5c9, defaults of 10 rounds and 180 seconds at lines 30 and 32). Those caps stop the loop. They do not skip model calls by running a model-written controller until hand-back. Any later work on this idea belongs in Ion, stays held, and does not merge with the held evaluation items.
